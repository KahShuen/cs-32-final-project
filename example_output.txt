MEETUP TOOL — STEP-BY-STEP TRACE
==================================
Input: example_input.json


STEP 1: load_users
-------------------
Read 5 users from JSON:
  Alice   — wants to meet: Bob       | free: Mon 10-12, Wed 14-17
  Bob     — wants to meet: Charlie   | free: Mon 09-11:30, Wed 15-17
  Charlie — wants to meet: Alice     | free: Mon 11-13, Wed 14:30-16
  David   — wants to meet: Eve       | free: Tue 13-15, Thu 10-12
  Eve     — wants to meet: David     | free: Tue 14-16, Thu 09-11


STEP 2: build_graph
--------------------
Convert wants_to_meet into undirected edges:
  Alice -> Bob       becomes  Alice -- Bob
  Bob -> Charlie     becomes  Bob -- Charlie
  Charlie -> Alice   becomes  Charlie -- Alice  (already exists)
  David -> Eve       becomes  David -- Eve
  Eve -> David       becomes  Eve -- David  (already exists)

Resulting adjacency dict:
  "Alice":   {"Bob", "Charlie"}
  "Bob":     {"Alice", "Charlie"}
  "Charlie": {"Alice", "Bob"}
  "David":   {"Eve"}
  "Eve":     {"David"}


STEP 3: find_groups  [MAIN COMPUTATION — BFS]
----------------------------------------------
Start BFS from Alice (unvisited):
  Visit Alice  → neighbors: Bob, Charlie
  Visit Bob    → neighbors: Alice (visited), Charlie
  Visit Charlie → neighbors: Alice (visited), Bob (visited)
  No more to visit → Group 1: {Alice, Bob, Charlie}

David is unvisited. Start BFS from David:
  Visit David → neighbors: Eve
  Visit Eve   → neighbors: David (visited)
  No more to visit → Group 2: {David, Eve}

All nodes visited. Done.
Result: [{"Alice", "Bob", "Charlie"}, {"David", "Eve"}]
